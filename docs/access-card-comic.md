Panel 2: the same operative presents his new card to a glowing green wall reader beside a sliding security door and steps through into the operations room. This is the actual opening story sequence: receive access card, enter operations, begin first intercept.

## Playable sequence

- Walk to reception and interact to see the comic. Accept the card to unlock room travel.
- Enter Operations, read the briefing, and complete the University Testbed intercept.
- Extract at or above the target's key goal, then stand up to report completion.
- Continue through Metro, Government, and Blacksite assignments, alternating between Operations and the Communications Centre.
- Access and completed assignments are saved locally. Failed or insufficient extractions do not advance progress; replaying an earlier target cannot advance the current assignment.
